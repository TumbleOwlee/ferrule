**Design checks** — before this stop is shown, every time it is reached (again after a `change`, and on a resumed run), as a loop of at most 3 check runs:

1. After every architect `ready`, run `ferrule gate ready {slug} <arch|epic>` first, so the previous results never outlive a `change`; after a pass-back add `--fixed <IDs|->` and `--needs-user <IDs|->`, copied from the architect's `ready` line. A resumed run already waiting at the stop reruns the checks without it.
2. Then spawn two `fe-checker` agents in parallel, briefs `scope=comprehension` and `scope=zoom-out`, each naming `{run_dir}`, `{run_dir}/spec-diff.md` (`none` in a non-behavior run), `{run_dir}/architecture.md` and `{run_dir}/check-loop.md`. Wait for both hand-offs; one that fails or returns nothing is not respawned in that check run.
3. Run `ferrule gate next {slug} checks <both hand-off lines, or status=none>`. On `next=pass-back`, relay its `do:` line verbatim to the same architect, then back to step 1 with its `ready`. On `next=stop`: at the architecture stop, follow its `run:` line; at the epic stop, follow `epic.md` steps 3–4 instead (issue draft first, `--issue <n>` on the render after `reuse`).

Hence the checks add no stop of their own: the stop comes after at most 3 check runs, and says `did not run` for a check without a current result. A run whose architecture is skipped, or whose brief is approved at gate 2's stop, runs neither check.
